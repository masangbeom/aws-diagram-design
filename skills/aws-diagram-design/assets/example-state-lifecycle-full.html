Step Functions batch job lifecycle

Five primary phases of a Step Functions batch job stay readable as one rail. The task-token approval and Retry policy leave the rail without becoming phases, while StopExecution and exhausted retries remain explicit terminal outcomes.

THE HEADLINE

Progress owns the primary rail

Submitted through Succeeded answers the operator's first question: where is this execution now? Supporting behavior stays visible without breaking that scan.

Wait and recovery are not phases

  • Approval pauses on a task token
  • Retry returns to Running
  • Dashed paths mark detours

Every stop names its cause

  • Cancelled is a StopExecution call
  • Failed means MaxAttempts exhausted
  • Text and dashes carry meaning
