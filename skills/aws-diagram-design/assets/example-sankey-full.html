EC2 instance-hours · a month of fleet capacity

12,000 Amazon EC2 instance-hours split across four workloads, then merge into three utilization outcomes. Band thickness is the data — the idle path is the only colored ribbon, because it's the line item worth cutting.

EDITORIAL POINT

Idle instances

1,000 of the 12,000 monthly instance-hours — about 8% of the fleet — are instances running at near-zero CPU: batch nodes left up after their jobs finish and web capacity held overnight. That's the one line item this diagram argues for cutting.

Where the hours go

Batch and the web tier together are three-quarters of the fleet (9,200 of 12,000 hours). CI runners and dev/test are comparatively small at 2,000 and 800.

Net outcome

9,400 hours do real work, and 1,600 are deliberate headroom for traffic peaks. The remaining 1,000 are pure overhead — neither work nor insurance, just instances nobody stopped.
