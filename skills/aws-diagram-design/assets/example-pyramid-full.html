AWS migration pyramid · effort vs. value

Three migration strategies from the AWS 7 Rs plus a reinvent tier, ordered by effort and the business value they unlock. The base exits the data center fast. The apex is where the portfolio actually changes what the business can do.

THE HEADLINE

One orange layer, on purpose

A four-color pyramid is a children's diagram. Reserving orange for the apex makes the structure say something: reinventing on Bedrock is the rarest move in the portfolio, and the rehost savings below are what fund it.

Counts carry the scale

  • ~180 apps rehosted with AWS MGN
  • ~80 replatformed onto RDS and Fargate
  • ~32 refactored to serverless
  • ~8 reinvented with generative AI

Pyramids only work for hierarchy

Retire, retain, relocate, and repurchase have no effort order against these four, so they stay off the pyramid. Pyramids promise you're trading volume for value as you climb.
