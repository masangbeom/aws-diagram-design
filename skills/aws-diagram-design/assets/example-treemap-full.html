Annual AWS spend · six services

Unblended AWS spend by service for fiscal 2026. Amazon EC2 takes $4.78 million — 59% of the bill, and more than the other five services put together.

One cell carries the story

EC2 is the focal cell because it is the finding, not because it sorts first. Nearly six of every ten dollars on the bill are inside that rectangle, so rightsizing and Graviton start here.

How to read it

Cells are laid out squarified, largest first, so aspect ratios stay near 1 and areas stay comparable by eye. Among the other five, stronger contrast means larger.

What the chart doesn't show

Spend, not growth. Bedrock's cell is under a tenth of EC2's today, and it is the one growing fastest — this is one year, not a direction.
