Where a workload's guardrails come from

How AWS Organizations scopes compose into one effective policy. Each outer ring is a broader boundary; the innermost VPC is where the workload runs — inheriting every SCP, account baseline, and Region control above it.

THE HEADLINE

Containment is the policy

A service control policy attached to the root or an OU wraps every account below it. Moving an account between OUs is the only change needed to swap its whole guardrail set.

Evaluates outside-in

  • Root SCPs apply first
  • Each OU narrows what is allowed
  • Account IAM grants within that ceiling
  • An explicit deny always wins

OUs nest five deep, max

AWS Organizations allows five levels of OUs under the root. If you need more, the structure is modeling teams, not risk — flatten it and use tags.
