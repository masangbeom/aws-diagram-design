Throughput vs. unit cost · 12 ECS services

Each point is an Amazon ECS service on Fargate. Moving right means more requests per second; moving down means each million requests costs less. Checkout occupies the lower-right — the ideal quadrant.

LOWEST UNIT COST

Checkout · (20K, $2)

20K requests/sec at $2 per million. Checkout runs on Graviton Fargate tasks behind an ALB, scales on request count per target, and keeps its hot reads in ElastiCache instead of Aurora.

Correlation is clear

Every service to the right of the median throughput (8K req/sec) is also below the median unit cost ($12). The relationship isn't causal — fixed costs like the ALB hour and minimum task count simply spread over more requests.

Two outliers worth examining

The service at (2K, $20) serves little traffic on capacity sized for peak — a minimum of six tasks that sit idle. The service at (12K, $10) is busy but pricier than expected — likely NAT Gateway data processing on every outbound call.
