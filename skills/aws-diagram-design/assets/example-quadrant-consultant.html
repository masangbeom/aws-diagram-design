THE HEADLINE

Name every future

Scenario planning fails when three of the four cells are placeholders. Forcing a name on each future — even the "won't happen" ones — surfaces the assumptions hiding in the axes.

The path to 02

  • Up first: move self-run clusters and databases to Fargate, Aurora, and other managed services
  • Then right: CloudWatch anomaly detection and Systems Manager Automation runbooks
  • Last: Amazon Q Developer investigations and Bedrock agents acting behind change approvals

Why 04 is the trap

AIOps on self-managed EC2 automates toil that managed services would remove. The models end up tuned to patch cycles and node failures the team could have handed to AWS.
