AWS Cloud Center of Excellence map

A responsibility map for a Cloud Center of Excellence running an AWS landing zone. Unlike a generic tree, it makes the intake front door, capability owners, specialist roles, and staffing gaps visible.

THE HEADLINE

One front door

Every AWS request lands in #ccoe-intake, and the CCoE Lead routes it to a pod. Application teams never hunt for the right specialist. The orange box is the only place work enters.

Pods own capabilities

  • Platform — landing zone, account vending with Control Tower and AFT
  • Security — IAM guardrails, GuardDuty findings
  • Data & AI — Glue, Redshift, SageMaker AI, Bedrock
  • FinOps — CUR, Cost Explorer, Savings Plans

Gaps stay on the chart

Data & AI has no on-call rotation, Bedrock quota increases are still waiting on approval, and nobody owns the tag policy that FinOps reports depend on. The dashed band keeps that setup work visible until a pod takes it.
