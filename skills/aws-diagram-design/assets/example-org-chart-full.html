AWS Cloud Center of Excellence map

A responsibility map for a Cloud Center of Excellence running an AWS landing zone. Unlike a generic tree, it makes the intake front door, capability owners, specialist roles, and staffing gaps visible.

Use when

The reader needs to know who owns what, where to route work, and which roles are active vs. still being wired.

Keep nodes short

Name, invocation path, and 2–4 scope words. Put detailed operating rules below the figure.

Show gaps

Unavailable owners and missing routes should be dashed, not hidden. They are operationally useful.
