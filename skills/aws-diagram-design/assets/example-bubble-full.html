Payments burns the error budget fastest

2.8% of 760 invocations per second — the highest error rate on the board applied to nearly the biggest volume, most of it throttles against the function's reserved concurrency. The accent marks the risk, not the worst number in any one column: Reports has worse duration and Router has more traffic, but neither multiplies out to as many failing requests.

Router shows where mass should sit

The largest bubble sits in the bottom-left corner — 900 invocations per second at 85ms and half a percent of errors. Area is what keeps that reading honest: 900 is fifteen times Reports' 60, and the bubble carries fifteen times the ink. Radius-proportional sizing would square that into a 225-to-one blot.

Reports is slow and it does not matter

460ms p95 — the worst latency here — on 60 invocations per second. The x-position raises the alarm and the tiny area answers it. Cropping the size encoding out of this chart turns Reports into the headline; that is the lie of every latency-only ranking.
