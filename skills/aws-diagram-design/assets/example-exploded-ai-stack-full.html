Generative AI agent on AWS · What sits under the prompt

Five layers pulled apart along one axis. You type at the top, AgentCore Runtime calls the Bedrock model, and Knowledge Bases decide what the answer is grounded in.

The headline

Your data is the layer you own

Models on Bedrock and the runtime around them change every few months. A knowledge base is your documents in S3, chunked and embedded into S3 Vectors, so it carries over when the rest of the stack is swapped.

Reading the explode

  • Top layer is where you type
  • The dark chip is the Bedrock model inside the runtime
  • Each card on the knowledge layer is one data source
  • Plugs on the tools layer are the Lambda functions and APIs it can call

Why identity is at the bottom

Every tool call runs on a scoped IAM role or an OAuth token brokered by AgentCore Identity. Keep it under everything else so no layer above holds a raw secret.
