AWS budget bridge · FY25 → FY26

How a 240 $k/mo AWS baseline becomes a 236 $k/mo plan. Two costs rise, two fall — and the running total carries across every gap, so the walk has to reconcile.

LARGEST SAVING

S3 tiering · −52

Moving cold datasets to S3 Intelligent-Tiering and Glacier Deep Archive claws back more than storage grew all year. It lands late in the walk on purpose — the carry shows the budget was above baseline until this one step.

The walk reconciles

240 + 64 − 38 + 22 − 52 = 236. Every bridge spans exactly its two running levels, and each carry line transports the total across the gap at that level — the chart is checkable arithmetic, not decoration.

What the bridge doesn't show

Each bar is a net figure. EC2 growth's +64 contains both new On-Demand capacity and one team's move to Spot; the walk states contributions, not the churn inside them.
