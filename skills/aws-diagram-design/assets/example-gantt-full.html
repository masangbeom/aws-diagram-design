Q2 migration wave · 12-week plan

Three MAP phases from assess through migrate. The cutover review in week 8 is the critical gate — the wave 1 rehost doesn't cut over until sign-off.

CRITICAL GATE

Cutover review — week 8

AWS MGN replication for wave 1 begins in week 7, but cutover is contingent on the go/no-go review. Starting replication before the gate means the team can fix launch-template issues early, not re-sync from scratch post-review.

Assess ↔ Mobilize overlap

The TCO business case (W2–W3) overlaps with the landing zone build (W3–W4) by one week. This is intentional — the first discovery findings size the AWS Control Tower accounts without a formal handoff ceremony.

Float in the schedule

Hypercare (W10–W11) starts in the last week of wave 1 (W10), and W12 is left open. If the rehost slips by one week, that open week absorbs it. If it slips two weeks, the data center exit date moves — there is no buffer beyond that.
