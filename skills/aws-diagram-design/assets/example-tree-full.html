AWS Organizations OU hierarchy

Three tiers, one root. Every member account in the AWS organization descends through exactly one organizational unit, so service control policies inherit predictably. Orthogonal connectors, one coral accent, nothing else.

THE HEADLINE

One root, one coral dot

An organization is a promise: every account has exactly one parent OU. Coral lives on the root because the management account and its SCPs govern everything below — the rest is a descent.

Connectors are orthogonal

  • Parent drops vertical
  • Horizontal bus connects siblings
  • Each child drops into its top edge
  • No diagonals, no curves

Uneven breadth is honest

Security and Infrastructure fan to two accounts; Workloads drops to one. The layout reflects the real shape of the organization, not a forced symmetry.
