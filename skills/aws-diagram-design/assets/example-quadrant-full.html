THE HEADLINE

One orange dot, on purpose

A 2×2 with four colored quadrants is a poster, not a decision tool. Reserving orange for the single item you commit to tomorrow — the Compute Savings Plan — makes the matrix actually prioritize.

Axes labeled at ends

  • HIGH / LOW at the extremes
  • Arrows show direction
  • No labels at midpoints
  • The cross is 1px ink, not a box

Avoid isn't empty by accident

Two items in "Avoid" is informative — it says the team considered self-hosting CI and Kafka and chose managed services instead. A blank quadrant would leave you wondering.
